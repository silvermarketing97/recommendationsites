Recommendation Sites is an independent blog that helps people in the **United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia and Ireland** earn extra money, online and offline, without falling for hype.

## What we cover

- **Paid surveys and rewards apps.** Which ones actually pay, how much to expect, and how to qualify for more surveys.
- **Swagbucks.** Our top pick for beginners, with step-by-step guides to earning more points.
- **Online income.** Freelancing, selling, app testing and other legit ways to earn from a laptop or phone.
- **Offline side hustles.** Local, real-world gigs you can start this weekend.
- **Money tips.** Ways to keep more of what you earn and avoid scams.

## How we work

We read the terms, try the platforms, and compare our notes with what real users report. We never promise specific earnings, because results depend on your country, your profile and the time you put in. When a platform has downsides, we tell you.

## How we make money

Some links on this site are referral or affiliate links, including our Swagbucks link. If you sign up through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This never changes what we write. Read our full [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).
