This policy explains how **Recommendation Sites** ("we", "us") handles information when you visit recommendationsites.com.

## Information we collect

- **Usage data.** Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) in server logs for security and performance. If we enable analytics (such as Google Analytics), it collects similar, aggregated data using cookies.
- **Information you send us.** If you email us, we receive your email address and message.

We do not sell your personal information.

## Cookies and third parties

Pages may load content from third parties, such as fonts from Google Fonts, images from Unsplash and videos from YouTube (videos load only when you press play). When you click a referral link, the destination site (for example Swagbucks) may set its own cookies to track the referral. Their privacy policies apply on their sites.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the UK and Ireland, the CCPA in California, PIPEDA in Canada or the Privacy Act in Australia) you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data. Contact us at {{ site.contactEmail }}.

## Children

This site is intended for adults. Most paid survey platforms require members to be at least 18 (some accept 13+ with restrictions).

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
