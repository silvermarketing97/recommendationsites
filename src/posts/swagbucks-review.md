## What is Swagbucks?

Swagbucks is a rewards platform that pays you points, called **SB**, for everyday online activities. You can collect SB by answering surveys, shopping online through its portal, answering daily polls, trying apps and games, and scanning receipts. When you have enough points, you exchange them for **PayPal cash or gift cards** from stores such as Amazon and Walmart.

As a rule of thumb, **100 SB is worth about $1**. So a survey that pays 150 SB is worth around $1.50.

Swagbucks makes its money from the brands and market research companies that pay to reach its members. That is why joining is free. **You should never pay to take surveys**, on Swagbucks or anywhere else.

## The best ways to earn on Swagbucks

### 1. Paid surveys

Surveys are the core of Swagbucks. Each one shows the reward in SB and an estimated time. Short surveys might pay well under a dollar, while longer or more targeted studies can pay a few dollars.

**How to earn more from surveys:**

- Compare the **SB per minute**, not just the total. A 5-minute survey for 75 SB beats a 25-minute survey for 200 SB.
- Answer honestly and consistently. Survey companies include check questions, and inconsistent answers can get you screened out.
- Check surveys at different times of day. New studies are added throughout the day.
- Accept that disqualifications happen. When you are screened out early, you sometimes receive a small consolation reward.

### 2. Shopping cashback

If you already shop online, this is the easiest money on Swagbucks. Click through Swagbucks to a partner store and you earn SB on qualifying purchases. During big sale events stores often boost their cashback rates.

<div class="tip">

Never buy something just for the cashback. The best strategy is to route purchases you were already going to make through Swagbucks.

</div>

### 3. Daily goals and polls

Swagbucks gives you small daily tasks, such as answering the daily poll or hitting a daily earning target. They take a minute and can add a bonus on top of what you earn elsewhere.

### 4. App, game and sign-up offers

These usually pay the most per task. You might earn SB for installing a game and reaching a certain level, or for signing up for a free trial. Read the terms carefully: note the deadline, whether a purchase is required, and cancel any free trial you do not want before it renews.

### 5. Magic Receipts

In supported countries you can earn SB on selected grocery and household products by scanning your receipt in the app. It is small money, but it is passive if you do it while unpacking your shopping.

## How much can you really make on Swagbucks?

This depends on your country, your profile and how much time you put in, so be wary of anyone promising specific numbers. Here is a realistic way to think about it:

| Your approach | Typical result |
|---|---|
| A few surveys a week, occasional cashback | A gift card every month or two |
| Daily surveys + daily goals + cashback on regular shopping | Roughly $20 to $50 a month |
| All of the above + selected high-paying offers | Can reach $100+ in good months |

The members who earn the most treat Swagbucks as a **side income for spare moments**: a few surveys while watching TV, cashback on purchases they already planned, and the occasional well-paying offer.
